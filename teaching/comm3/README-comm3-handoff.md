Everything in this folder was built with Andrew in chat. Nothing here is a guess; where something is undecided it is listed as open. Formatting rules for anything generated from these: no em dashes, no emojis, no font size hierarchy, Andrew's voice.

**Files**

1. comm3-calendar.md and comm3-calendar.json. The dated schedule. Every meeting date with its one topic or question, workshop days, no-class days, and every due date. This is the piece to load into the online schedule. The JSON has the same content with a kind field per day: class, workshop, no_class, break, due.
2. comm3-course-plan.md. The full plan: course frame, outcomes, week by week, assignments and grading, story bank summary, and open items. Longer than the calendar and includes reasoning.
3. comm3-one-page.md. A colleague-facing summary in Andrew's voice.
4. story-bank.html. 113 candidate stories, ten or more per skill week, each with what happened, why it teaches the skill, what to show, and the move in class. Has a rating widget; ratings live in browser localStorage, not in the file.
5. comm3-story-ratings.md. Andrew's ratings of the bank, exported. Greats are the working set, goods are the bench.
6. instructor-audio-feedback-spec.md. Separate feature spec, already handed to Claude Code earlier. Not part of the schedule.
